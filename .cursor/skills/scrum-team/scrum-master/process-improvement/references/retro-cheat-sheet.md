# Retro cheat sheet

## Timebox (60 min team)

| Segment | Minutes |
|---------|---------|
| Set the stage | 5 |
| Gather data | 15 |
| Insights | 15 |
| Decide actions | 15 |
| Close | 5 |
| Buffer | 5 |

## Working agreements (examples)

- One conversation at a time
- Assume positive intent
- Share airtime
- Confidential within the team (unless agreed otherwise)
- Actions must be owned

## Action quality bar

Bad: “Communicate better.”  
Good: “Daily 10-min standup ends with explicit blocker owner; SM tracks blockers older than 48h.”
