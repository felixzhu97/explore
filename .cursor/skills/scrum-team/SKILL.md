# Scrum Team

Scrum 只承认这三类 **Accountabilities**（同属一个 Scrum Team）：

| Accountability | 目录 | 职责 |
|----------------|------|------|
| **Product Owner** | [`product-owner/`](./product-owner/) | 产品 Backlog、价值最大化、验收 |
| **Scrum Master** | [`scrum-master/`](./scrum-master/) | 流程、教练、障碍、持续改进 |
| **Developers** | [`developers/`](./developers/) | 每个 Sprint 交付可用增量（含测试与质量） |

团队外的战略与支撑角色见 [`../stakeholders/`](../stakeholders/)、[`../supporting/`](../supporting/)。
