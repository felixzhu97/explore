# Sponsorship

## When to use

- 新立项 / 续费 / 砍项目
- 澄清「谁掏钱、谁拍板、失败找谁」
- 需要越过团队的组织级障碍

## Sponsor one-pager

```text
Initiative:
Problem / opportunity:
Why now:
Funding envelope: <amount / capacity> for <period>
Success criteria (business):
Out of scope:
Decision rights: Sponsor decides X; PO decides Y
Escalation: <when> → Sponsor within <SLA>
Review cadence: <e.g. monthly>
```

## Anti-patterns

- Sponsor 直接改 Sprint 任务
- 有预算无成功标准
- 多个「赞助人」互相否决且无裁决机制
